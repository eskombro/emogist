The code is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The data has several origins, so each part keeps the most open terms it can.

|---|---|

| **AI rows** (`source` = `ai`): sentences generated by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or, in the v2 dev and test sets only, gpt-oss 120B, and their emoji labels | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), to the extent any rights exist |

These rules apply to the v1 data in `03_dataset/` (`full/`, `pilot/`, `labels/`, `review/`, `sentences/`), the v2 dev and test sets in `08_v2_spanish_and_eval_sets/sets/`, and the v2 training data in `09_v2_dataset_and_labels/` (`train_main.jsonl`, `train_control.jsonl`, `sentences/`, `labels/`, `review_sheet.csv`). In files without a `source` column, IDs starting with `ai` (`ai-`, `ai2-`, `ai2t-`) are AI rows and all others are real rows.

The v2 dev and test sets contain no text written by Claude, so they follow the row licences above. Please don't train on them anyway: they only stay useful as benchmarks while no model has seen them.

## Using real rows

Credit the sentence's author and Tatoeba, link to the licence, and state any changes. For example:

> Sentence by `<author>`, from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR. Emoji label added by the Emogist project (AI-generated).

Authors are in the `author` field of the training files (`train.jsonl`, `train_main.jsonl`, `train_control.jsonl`), of the v2 dev and test sets, and of the real sentence pools (`03_dataset/sentences/`, `08_v2_spanish_and_eval_sets/sets/`, `09_v2_dataset_and_labels/sentences/`). The sentence text is unchanged from Tatoeba. The emoji labels are treated as adaptations, so they stay under the same licence.

## Evaluation material

These files contain text written by Claude (Anthropic): the v1 test sentences drafted with Claude, the blind-review sentences, decodes and judgements. They are published so the results can be checked and reproduced. **They are not licensed for training AI models**, because Anthropic's terms restrict that use. Tatoeba sentences that appear in them remain under CC BY 2.0 FR.
