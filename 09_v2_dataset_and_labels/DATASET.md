# Emogist training dataset, v2

6,000 everyday sentences in English, French and Spanish, each paired with an AI-generated emoji sentence that keeps its gist. Built in [step 9](README.md); used to train the v2 model ([step 10](../10_v2_fine_tuning/)).

# Main features and functionalities

- **Balanced:** 2,000 examples per language, 1,000 real and 1,000 AI-generated.
- **Real sentences** from Tatoeba, with author and sentence ID kept for attribution.
- **AI sentences** written directly in each language, in eight patterns, including questions, conditionals and exact numbers.
- **Emoji labels** from one teacher model and one prompt version, all passing the v2 format and convention checks.

- **Emoji labels:** every label is **AI-generated** by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, using a prompt with 43 examples written by the author (EN 16, FR 14, ES 13). DeepSeek's terms allow training other models on its outputs, as long as published AI content is labelled as such.

## How it was made

1. **Real sentences** were drawn in a fixed shuffled order from the step 8 pool, with quotas for questions and long sentences and at most 10% per Tatoeba author (questions excepted). Sentences from v1 data, v1 and v2 dev and test sets, their near-duplicates and the worksheet were left out.
2. **AI sentences** came from a grid of patterns, topics and speakers, with blocklist, format and near-duplicate checks.
3. **Labels:** two tries at temperature 0.3, then up to two repairs in which Flash sees its broken label and a one-line description of the broken rule. The code never suggests emojis. The only automatic edit is a space added before a ❓ glued to another emoji.
4. **Checks:** v2 format (1 to 10 emojis) and conventions (❓ on its own, ❔ 📍 🔁 attached, ❓ exactly when the sentence has a "?", no copy of an example label, no 🔀 or 🔄). A label that fails is retried. The prompt also says 🫵 is only "you", but that is not checked. 74 sentences got no label and were replaced from a 3% margin.

No sentence overlaps with the v2 dev and test sets, exactly or as a near-duplicate. Real sentences also exclude everything v1 used.

## Limitations

- Automatic checks verify **format, not meaning**, so a valid label can still be poor.
- **Sample review:** the author judged 13% of 150 labels wrong before the final prompt, then 7% of 60 after it (roughly 3% to 16%). No rows were removed: the review is a measurement.
- **🫵 is still overused:** 293 labels use it with no "you" word in the sentence, partly imperatives and Spanish sentences without a pronoun, partly real misuses.
- One Tatoeba contributor wrote 218 of the 3,000 real sentences.
- Real and AI sentences differ in length and style: AI sentences are almost twice as long.
- The Spanish person filter misses sentences without a subject pronoun, so Spanish real sentences lean towards ones that name a person.
- Tags come from word lists: `speech` undercounts, especially in French, and the numbers rule can misfire.
- The blocklist is simple, so it can miss unsuitable sentences and remove harmless ones.
